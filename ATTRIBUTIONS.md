# Attributions

Third-party creative assets bundled in YLaPiano.

## Piano sound — Upright Piano KW (FreePats project)

- **What:** `Ylapiano/Resources/Audio/UprightPianoKW-small-20190703.sf2` — the
  app's piano instrument (B23, #34). Every piano note the app plays or renders
  comes from this SoundFont.
- **Source:** FreePats project,
  <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html#UprightKW>
  (`UprightPianoKW-small-SF2-20190703.7z`).
- **Authors:** Recorded from a Kawai upright piano by Gonzalo
  (humanogonzalo@gmail.com) and Roberto (roberto@zenvoid.org), January 2017;
  edited and processed by Roberto.
- **License:** [CC0 1.0 public domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  — no attribution legally required. Credited here anyway (and in the app's
  Grown-Ups corner once B17 ships) because it's the decent thing to do.

### Why not Salamander

The plan originally named "Salamander C5 Light SF2" as CC-BY 3.0. Verified
2026-07-04: that light SF2 is a HEDSound derivative under **CC-BY-NC 4.0** —
non-commercial, unusable in a paid app. Alexander Holm's original Salamander
Grand Piano (CC-BY 3.0) is only distributed as a 296 MB SF2 / 707 MB SFZ,
far over the ≤20 MB in-bundle budget. Upright Piano KW is a real sampled
piano at 9.5 MB under CC0 — better license, budget fits, sounds like a real
instrument in a living room, which suits a kids' piano.
