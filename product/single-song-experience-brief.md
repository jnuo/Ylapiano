# Brief — The "Play One Song" Experience (Salta l'Esquirol)

**Producer:** Anya · **Date:** 2026-06
**Mission (Onur):** make playing _one song_ **super fun**, and make a player —
kid (5–7) **and** adult — **measurably better at that song** each time they play.
Make it as good as we can, bounded to this one experience.

## Vertical slice

ONE song, end to end: open → lead-in → play (falling notes + real keys) →
live feedback → result → replay, _better next time._ No new songs, no new
modes. This slice IS the proof.

## The audience just widened — flag

"Kids **and** adults" is two skill ceilings, not one. A 5-year-old needs a
floor they can't fail through; an adult needs a ceiling that rewards mastery.
**One difficulty cannot serve both** — the experience must _scale_ (gentle
entry, rising challenge). Reconciling that is Defne's first call, not a vibe.

## What "much better" means (measurable, or it's not real)

Across replays of THIS song, in one sitting: **missed notes go down / stars go
up** for the same player. That curve — for a kid _and_ an adult — is the proof
we're teaching, not just entertaining. (Owner of the metric: Mei.)

## Work packages — who does what

- **Defne (design) — spine, goes first.** The complete loop _and_ the learning
  curve: how the same song gets a 5yo and an adult to "play it better" — tempo/
  difficulty scaling, guided→unguided, near-miss-driven practice, beat-your-best.
- **Marco (tech).** Timing/feedback accuracy (lead-in + sync = done); add what
  "play better" needs — slow-practice tempo, accurate per-note hit/miss data.
- **Diego (motion).** Juice on the fun moments + the win celebration; honors
  reduced-motion. Mostly built; polish to Defne's loop.
- **Khalid (sound).** The song's notes correct + teaching-clear; hit sparkle;
  result sound. Decide melody-as-guide vs kid-plays with Defne.
- **Aiko (art).** Result/mascot art — **parked** until the loop proves out
  (then OpenArt prompts). Not the fun lever.
- **Mei (playtest).** The gate. A kid AND an adult both (a) want to replay,
  (b) measurably improve at this song over a few rounds.
- **Anya.** Sequence, hold the bar, backlog the rest.

## Sequence

1. **Defne** designs the complete kid+adult experience (now).
2. **Marco + Diego + Khalid** execute their lanes against her design (parallel).
3. **Mei** validates against the bar (the gate).
4. **Aiko** polishes art once the loop is proven.

## Quality gate / kill criteria

Ship-this-as-good-as-we-can = Mei's playtest shows, for a 5yo _and_ an adult:
replay unprompted **and** a downward miss-curve across rounds on this song.
If the kid won't replay, fix the fun before the teaching. If they replay but
don't improve, the learning design is wrong — back to Defne.

## Parked (don't drop)

Mascot identity (Aiko), backing-track vs kid-plays, more songs, score HUD /
progression map — revisit only after this one song is proven great.
